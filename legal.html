Legal and privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

The Lesson Chunks Agent is free software you run yourself. There are no accounts, no cookies and no trackers beyond an anonymous page counter on this website. Your documents, answers and lessons never leave your machine except in the model call you choose to make.

Software licence

The agent is released under the PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 licence: free for families, schools, churches, charities and personal projects, with credit. Commercial use (paid tutoring businesses, resale, products built on it) needs an arrangement with The AI Champion. The lesson files the agent produces for you are yours.

Privacy

  • This website uses a cookieless, anonymous page-view counter. No personal data is collected, stored or shared.
  • The agent stores lesson sources, chunk plans, answers and lesson files in a folder you choose on your own machine.
  • Model calls: when you use an online model, the text of the lesson is sent to that provider under their terms. A local model keeps everything on your machine.
  • No student data is sent to The AI Champion, ever. There is nothing to breach because we hold nothing.

What this is not

The agent is a study aid. It is not a teacher, a diagnostician, a therapist or a legal service, and it does not promise outcomes: no grades, no diagnoses, no guarantees. Decisions about a young person's education belong to them, their family and their school. If a young person is in distress, involve a trusted adult; the agent points there too.

A note for schools: check your own policies on AI use before adopting any tool, including this one. The local-first design makes those conversations easier: no student data leaves the school's machines.
